# Dr. Farmer

**Instant crop & livestock disease diagnosis**

Dr. Farmer is an AI-powered agricultural advisory platform designed for Indian farmers. Simply snap a photo of any crop leaf or animal symptom, and get an AI-powered diagnosis along with home remedies and medical treatment options in seconds.

## Features

- **Crop Check**: Scan leaves for spots, rust, pests, and stem rot.
- **Cattle Check**: Monitor udder, skin, eyes, mouth, and hooves for livestock health.
- **Farm Record Book**: Keep a detailed log of past health checks, treatments, sprays, and reminders.
- **Voice Guide**: Get spoken audio advice in Hindi & English for easier accessibility.
